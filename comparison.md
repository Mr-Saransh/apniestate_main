# Feature Comparison & Roadmap

## 1. Current Features of Apni Estate (Web Application)

Based on the current architecture and pages, Apni Estate offers a comprehensive suite of construction management tools:

*   **Project Management**: Dashboard, Projects, Project Details, Sites, and DPR (Daily Progress Reports).
*   **Workforce Management**: Attendance tracking, Leaves management, Workers database, and Payroll processing.
*   **Task Management**: Creating, assigning, and tracking Tasks across different projects.
*   **Material & Inventory**: Materials tracking and Inventory management.
*   **Financial Control**: Budgets, Finance tracking, Invoices, Payments, and Payroll.
*   **Stakeholder Management**: Contractors, Users (role-based access), and Vendors.
*   **Documentation & Admin**: Documents storage, Reports generation, Notifications, Onboarding, and Settings.
